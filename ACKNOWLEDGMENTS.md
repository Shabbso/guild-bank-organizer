# Acknowledgments

Guild Bank Organizer is an independent addon with no required dependencies.

Its user-facing ordering is intentionally familiar to players who use default
bag sorting: quality, item class/subclass, equipment slot, item level, vendor
value, and name. ElvUI's Mists bag-sort behavior informed the compatibility
target, and BankStack was reviewed as an established example of guild-bank
movement safeguards.

No ElvUI or BankStack source files are bundled with this addon, and neither
project is required at runtime. Smart Deposit's compact item-era correction
ranges are generated from Blizzard client DB2 data served by Wago Tools; Wago
Tools is not contacted by the addon and is not required at runtime.
